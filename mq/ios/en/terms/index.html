2. What you get

Motivation Quotes is an app with quotes from well-known people, films and books. Part of it is free, the rest opens with a subscription.

Free, no subscription: the "Editor's picks" collection – 300 quotes in English, 270 in Russian – plus notifications and favorites built from those quotes.

With a subscription: the whole library – all topics and authors, also in the filters – and any quote in your favorites and notifications. Today that is nearly 3,000 quotes in English and more than 9,000 in Russian. The library grows over time, and everything in it is included.

All quotes ship inside the app. There is no account and nothing to log into: the subscription is tied to the Apple Account that bought it.

3. The subscription

  • The trial starts when you confirm the purchase. Nothing is charged during it.
  • Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it turns into a paid year and the payment is taken then.
  • After that the subscription renews automatically every year, and Apple charges the payment method on your Apple Account within 24 hours before each renewal.
  • The price may change over time. Apple asks for your consent before charging a higher price, and you can cancel instead.

5. Cancelling

Cancel any time: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Motivation Quotes → Cancel Subscription.

The cancellation stops the next renewal. Access stays until the end of the period you already paid for. The app confirms the subscription when it is online: without an internet connection access holds for 7 days after the last check, then subscription quotes stay locked until the app can check again.

Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in Settings.
