Privacy Policy

CardScanner for iOS · Revision 2026-09-10 · Effective 2026-09-10 · Русский

CardScanner recognises trading cards using your device's camera and keeps your collection on your device. In version 1.0 the app makes no network requests of its own, and the developer receives no photographs, no collection data and no usage data.

The short version. Point the camera at a card, the phone works out which card it is, and the answer is stored in a database on the phone. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. If you delete the app, the collection goes with it.

The camera

iOS asks for your permission before the app can use the camera, and you can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings. The app uses the camera for one thing: working out which card is in front of it. Frames are processed as they arrive and are not saved as photographs.

The app does not ask for access to your photo library, and cannot read it.

Your collection and device backups

Your collection lives in a database inside the app's storage. We do not exclude that file from Apple's system backup, so if you have iCloud Backup or computer backups switched on, your collection is included in those backups like other app data.

That backup is Apple's mechanism, not ours. It is governed by Apple's own privacy terms and by your device settings, and the developer has no access to it. We mention it because "stored only on your device" would otherwise be misleading.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single card from the collection screen in the app.
  • Delete the app to remove the collection database along with it.

Copies inside a backup you made earlier are removed according to your backup settings, not by deleting the app. There is no server-side copy for us to delete, because there is no server.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party software development kits.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data. There is none to sell.

Independently of this app, Apple may process information about installations and app usage under Apple's own terms and your device settings. That is between you and Apple; we do not receive those reports unless you have chosen to share them, and they do not identify you to us.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, children included, and has no social, messaging or user-generated content features.
