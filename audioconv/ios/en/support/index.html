What to put in your message

Almost every question turns on these four things, so please include them:

  • the app version and build – in the app, tap the gear in the Library, then look at Version under About;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you did, step by step, and where the file came from (Photos or Files);
  • what happened, and what you expected instead.

You do not have to attach the video or the audio. If you choose to, our mailbox is hosted by Google, so the attachment sits on Google's systems along with your e-mail address. The app itself does not send your files anywhere; sending one to us is your choice, not something we need.

If the request is complex or we receive several requests, we may need up to two additional months, and we will tell you why within the first month. See the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

How do I make a ringtone?

Open a video or audio file, tap Make ringtone, select up to 30 seconds, tap Use this part, then tap Save ringtone. AudioConv creates an M4A file. What comes next depends on your iOS version, and the app shows the right steps after saving.

iOS 26: tap Use as Ringtone. In the Share menu that opens, choose Use as Ringtone; if it is not there, first expand the list of actions.

iOS 17 and 18: on these versions iOS does not let an app set a ringtone directly, so it is done in GarageBand, Apple's app. These steps require a compatible version of GarageBand. If one is installed, or the App Store offers you a compatible download, follow the guide in the app – tap How to set the ringtone – or these steps:

  1. Tap Save to Files and choose On My iPhone.
  2. On the Home Screen, touch and hold the GarageBand icon and choose Create New Audio Recording.
  3. Tap Tracks. Before adding the file, tap the Song Section button on the right of the ruler, then the Inspector icon, and turn on Automatic.
  4. Select the track, tap the Loop Browser button, then Files → Browse items from the Files app. Pick your file, then hold it and drag it to the very start of the track.
  5. Use the Navigation button to go back to My Songs.
  6. Select the song, tap Share → Ringtone → Export. Then tap Use sound as → Standard Ringtone.

Item names in GarageBand may differ slightly between versions. Apple describes the same steps at support.apple.com/en-us/120692. If you cannot install GarageBand, you can still save or share the M4A.

I bought Premium, but the app does not show it

Tap the gear in the Library, then Restore Purchases; the Premium screen also has a Restore button. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you paid with. Restoring does not charge you again.

If the status shows Checking… for a long time, connect to the internet and tap Check purchases again. If Apple approves a pending purchase – for example, a parent through Family Sharing – Premium becomes available when AudioConv receives the updated purchase status. If needed, reopen the app and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, then your name (Apple Account), then Subscriptions → AudioConv → Cancel Subscription (Apple explains it at support.apple.com/en-us/118428). While Premium is active, the app's Settings also has a Manage Subscription button that opens the same Apple screen. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Deleting AudioConv does not cancel a subscription.

If you bought Lifetime while a subscription was running, the subscription is not cancelled automatically – cancel it in Manage Subscription.

Apple handles billing and refunds; ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We receive purchase records and Premium status, but do not receive your card details or issue refunds.

What is free, and what does Premium give?

Your first file is free, in full. It counts only when an extraction finishes and the file is saved; a cancelled or failed one does not use it up. Reinstalling the app is not a reliable way to get it back.

Weekly and yearly Premium give you unlimited extractions while the subscription is active and renew automatically at the price shown before purchase. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews. Files already in your Library stay yours to play, share and save without Premium.

Where are my files?

In the app's Library – the first screen. They are kept inside the app on your iPhone, so they do not appear in the Files app on their own. To get a file out, tap Share to send it anywhere, or Save to Files to put a copy in a folder you choose. Swipe a file to the left to rename or delete it. Deleting removes it from the app only; copies you saved to Files stay.

Your Library is eligible for iPhone backup (iCloud or computer); it can come back when you restore a backup that contains AudioConv's data. If you delete the app, the Library is deleted with it – save anything you want to keep to Files first. We keep no copy of your files and cannot recover them.

Which formats can I use?

  • What you can open: a video from Photos, or a video or audio file from Files and iCloud Drive. Files stored in iCloud are downloaded by iOS first. Files that are damaged or copy-protected cannot be opened, and a video without a sound track has nothing to extract.
  • What you get: MP3, M4A, WAV or AAC. For MP3, M4A and AAC you choose the quality: 128, 192 or 320 kbps (WAV is uncompressed and has no quality setting). Ringtones are always M4A, up to 30 seconds.
  • Trimming: keep just the part you need, with an optional smooth start and end (fade in and out).

Default format and quality for new files are set in the app's Settings, under Defaults.

The app says there is not enough storage

A video from Photos is copied into the app while it is being processed, and the new file needs room too, so long videos need free space. Free some up in Settings → General → iPhone Storage and try again.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. AudioConv converts your media on your iPhone and does not send your media, file names or paths to us, Adapty or Mixpanel. The app does send usage analytics to Mixpanel and purchase data to Adapty; analytics include where a file came from and rough ranges of its size and length. If you share a file, save it to a cloud folder or include it in a device backup, its contents and name may leave your iPhone. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what goes where, and how to ask us to delete it.

What we cannot help with

  • Your Apple Account, billing and refunds. These are handled by Apple; we cannot change them.
  • GarageBand and iOS settings. We can tell you the steps, but these are Apple's apps.
  • Content you do not have the right to use. Extract sound only from videos and files that you made or are allowed to use.
